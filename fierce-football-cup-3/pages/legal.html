Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 21 May, 2026
Our Commitment to Privacy

The EKSU Fresher's Cup 3.0 platform is built with your privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or submit any personal information to view tournament content.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information:

  • Feedback Form: If you choose to leave a review or feedback, we collect your name, email address, and message. This is entirely voluntary.
  • MOTM Votes: Votes are session-based and not linked to any personal identity.
  • Theme Preference: Your chosen display theme (dark/sepia/light) is saved locally in your browser only — we never see this.
2. How We Use Your Information

Any feedback or review submitted through the platform is sent directly to the technical team email for the purpose of improving the platform and addressing concerns. We will never sell, share, or use your information for any other purpose.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Web3Forms: Used to process feedback form submissions. Their privacy policy applies to information submitted through the form.
  • YouTube/Facebook: Used for live stream embedding. Their respective privacy policies apply when you interact with embedded streams.
  • Vercel: Our hosting platform. Standard web server logs may be maintained by Vercel per their privacy policy.
4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage only to save your theme preference and MOTM vote status. No tracking cookies are used on this platform. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Data Security

The admin panel is protected by secure credentials. We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of tournament data published on this platform.

6. Children's Privacy

This platform is intended for university students and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 16.
